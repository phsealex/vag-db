# Limitations
## Keyless-only Coding
Some codings are specific to keyless models, such as [Disable Key Warning](/audi-a4-b8/09%20-%20Central%20Electronics/07%20-%20Coding/lch.md#disable-key-warning)

To know if your Audi A4 is a keyless models there are some dotations you can look for in your car;

Your car may be keyless if:<br><br>

**MMI**: In `Car > Vehicle Settings > Central Locking` you have an option referencing `Convenience Key`<br>

**Car**: You have an Engine Start/Stop button near your shifter
